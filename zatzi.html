Overview

Zatzi is an agricultural advisory web app for smallholder farmers in South Sudan and South Africa. Most of my work on it has gone into a backend that can be trusted in production.

Backend hardening

The backend runs on Supabase (PostgreSQL) and is roughly 95% of the way to production readiness:

  • Atomic RPC functions for operations that update several tables at once and must never half-complete.
  • Rate limiting to protect the AI and database layers from abuse and runaway costs.
  • Structured logging and Sentry so errors in the field can be traced, not guessed at.
  • Apple in-app purchase handling for the iOS version.

The open question: who is the customer?

The hardest decision on Zatzi isn't technical. As a consumer app it competes directly with plant-identification apps like PictureThis and Plantix. As a business-to-government or agricultural-organisation platform, it sells to fewer, larger customers with different needs. The backend supports either, but the product, pricing and roadmap don't.

I'm resolving this before building further features, because building for both would make it weaker at each.

Status and next steps

Backend near production. Next: settle the positioning, then run a small pilot with farmers to measure whether the advice changes decisions.
