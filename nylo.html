Nylo

A curriculum-aligned learning platform for South Sudanese public schools, with an AI study assistant and a portal for every role in a child's education.

Overview

Nylo is a learning platform for pupils in South Sudanese public schools, covering the national curriculum from Primary 1 to Senior 4. It combines curriculum content with an AI study assistant, and gives each group involved in a child's education its own portal.

The problem

Public school pupils often share textbooks and have limited access to help outside the classroom. Most learning apps are built for other curricula, priced for families who can pay, and assume a strong connection. Nylo is built for the South Sudanese curriculum, is free for schools and pupils, and is designed to be used mainly in class.

How it is organised

PortalWho it servesWhat it is for
StudentPupils, P1 to S4Lessons, questions to the AI assistant, streaks and class standings
ParentFamiliesFollowing a child's progress
TeacherClass teachersSeeing how a class is progressing
MinistryMinistry of EducationAdoption and outcomes across schools

Key decisions

  • English only. The platform teaches in English on purpose, so pupils build their English alongside each subject.
  • Learning through play. Streaks and competition between classes keep pupils coming back, so a lesson competes with a game instead of losing to one.
  • The Ministry pays, not families. Schools and pupils get it free; the Ministry of Education is the intended customer.
  • Online first. Launching online tests demand before investing in offline use, which comes later.

Responsible design

Nylo will hold data about children. The design collects as little as possible, gives each role access only to what it needs, and treats the AI assistant's answers as a safety concern: an unsuitable answer to a child is not just a bug.

My contribution

I'm co-developing Nylo with one collaborator. I've led the product scope, the role structure and the brand system, and I'm preparing the pitch to the Ministry of Education.

Design prototype

An interactive iOS prototype of the student, parent and admin experiences. It was built under the app's earlier working name, Revise, so that name still appears on some screens.

Status and next steps

In development. Next: a working pilot for one year group, then the Ministry pitch, then expansion to curricula in other African countries, with South Sudan as the main focus.
